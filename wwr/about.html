Canyon Current Rafting

At Canyon Current Rafting, we believe every river has a story worth discovering. Our mission is to bring people together through safe, exciting, and memorable whitewater adventures. With experienced guides, a passion for the outdoors, and a commitment to teamwork, we help adventurers of all skill levels experience the beauty and thrill of the river.

History

Canyon Current Rafting began in 2014 when a small group of river guides turned their shared love of whitewater into a family-owned business. What started with two rafts and a handful of weekend trips has grown into a welcoming community of guides and adventurers. Today, we continue to share our passion for the river, one unforgettable journey at a time.
